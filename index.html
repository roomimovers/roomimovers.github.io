FAQ

Your questions, answered

How much does a move cost?

We charge by the hour with a two-hour minimum. Your total depends on home size, stairs, distance and heavy items. Get a free estimate and we'll give you your hourly rate, the time we expect it to take, and any extra charges before you book.

Do you bring the truck?

Yes. The box truck, moving blankets, stretch wrap, dollies and straps are included. Need help with your own rental truck instead? Choose labor only.

Can you move a gun safe or appliances?

Yes. Tell us the size and where it's going (garage, upstairs, etc.) so we bring the right equipment.

How far ahead should I book?

One to two weeks is ideal. Weekends and the last few days of the month book first, so reach out early if your date is fixed.

Which areas do you serve?

Tampa, Carrollwood, Wesley Chapel and Lutz, plus Brandon, Riverview, Temple Terrace, Westchase, Zephyrhills and the other areas listed on our service areas page. If you're close by, call us anyway.
