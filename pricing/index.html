How the clock works

  1. We arrive

    The clock starts when we arrive. You get a two-hour arrival window and updates on timing.

  2. We wrap, load and move

    We walk through with you, wrap and pad your furniture and get everything where it needs to go.

  3. The last item is placed

    The clock stops when the last item is placed. There is a two-hour minimum on every move.

What changes your total

Home size and stairs

A larger home takes more time, and stairs slow every trip. Tell us the rooms and the floors at both addresses.

Distance and long carries

The drive and the walk from the truck to your door both count. Tell us how far the truck will be from the door.

Heavy items

Items over 300 lbs, like gun safes, may add to the price. Send us the size and where it is going.

Package

Labor Only, Full-Service and White-Glove include different help, so choose the one that fits your move.
