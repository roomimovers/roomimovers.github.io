Guide: moving day

How to get ready for moving day

A little preparation makes moving day faster and calmer. Here is what to do before the truck arrives.

The day before

  • Finish packing and tape every box
  • Label each box with its room and contents
  • Pack an essentials bag with chargers, medications, toiletries and a change of clothes
  • Set aside documents, jewelry and other valuables to carry yourself

Make room for the truck

Save a parking spot close to the door if you can. If you live in an apartment or condo, reserve the elevator or loading area if your building requires it, and ask about move-in hours.

Clear the way

  • Move cars, bikes and boxes out of driveways and hallways
  • Keep pets and small children in a safe room
  • Open gates and have access codes ready for both addresses

Appliances and electronics

  • Empty and defrost the fridge and freezer ahead of time
  • Disconnect and drain washer hoses
  • Take a photo of how cables are connected before you unplug them

When we arrive

  • Walk through with us and point out fragile or high-value items
  • Tell us where each room at the new place is
  • Keep your essentials bag with you

Before we leave

Do a last walk through every room, closet and cabinet, and check that everything has been placed where you want it.
