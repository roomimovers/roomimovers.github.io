Guide: heavy items

Moving gun safes, appliances and other heavy items

Heavy items need a plan and the right equipment. Here is how to prepare, and what to tell us so we bring what the job needs.

Tell us three things

  • What it is and how large it is, with a photo if you can
  • About how much it weighs
  • Where it is going: garage, upstairs, a tight hallway

Items over 300 lbs, like gun safes, may add to the price. We tell you before you book.

Measure first

Measure the item, the doorways, the hallways and the stairs at both addresses. A piece that does not fit through a doorway cannot be moved through it.

Gun safes

  • Empty the safe before the move and carry its contents yourself
  • Make sure the path from the safe to the truck is clear
  • Tell us the weight and where it is going

Washers, dryers and refrigerators

  • Disconnect the water, power and drain lines ahead of time
  • Empty and defrost the fridge and freezer the day before
  • Secure or remove loose shelves and drawers

Treadmills and exercise equipment

  • Unplug and fold or lower the equipment if it is designed to
  • Remove loose parts and keep screws in a labeled bag

Oversized furniture

Take note of any piece that may need to come apart, and tell us which one. On full-service moves, beds are taken apart and set back up.
