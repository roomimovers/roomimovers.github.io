Guide: packing

A simple guide to packing

Good packing keeps your things safe and keeps your move moving. Start early and go room by room.

Gather supplies

  • Sturdy boxes in a few sizes
  • Packing paper or bubble wrap for fragile items
  • Strong tape and a marker
  • Bags or covers for mattresses

Pack room by room

Start with the rooms you use least. Keep every room in its own boxes so unpacking is quick.

Heavy things in small boxes

Books and tools go in small boxes. Lighter items, like linens and pillows, go in larger ones. A box you can lift comfortably is easier and safer to carry.

Wrap the fragile things

  • Wrap dishes one at a time and stand plates on their edges
  • Fill empty space so nothing shifts
  • Mark the box FRAGILE on the top and sides

Clothes, art and electronics

  • Leave clothes on hangers in wardrobe boxes or garment bags if you can
  • Keep TVs, art and mirrors upright, wrapped and protected
  • Photograph cable setups before unplugging

Label everything

Write the room and a short list of contents on two sides of each box, and number them so you can tell that every box arrived.

Do not pack

Keep flammables, cleaning chemicals and other hazardous materials out of the truck, and carry important papers, medications and valuables with you.
