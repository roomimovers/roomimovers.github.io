Help us plan your building

Tell us when you request your estimate:

  • Which floor you are on, at both places
  • Whether there is an elevator, and if it needs a reservation
  • Where the truck can park, and how far it is to your door
  • Any gate codes or building move-in rules

Stairs and long carries

Stairs and long walks from the truck can add time, so we ask about them up front and include them in your estimate.

Two-hour arrival window

We give you a two-hour arrival window and keep you posted on timing, so you are not waiting around all day.

How pricing works

  • Billed by the hour, with a two-hour minimum. The clock starts when we arrive and stops when the last item is placed.
  • Your free estimate shows the hourly rate and how long we expect the job to take.
  • Stairs, long carries from the truck and items over 300 lbs (like gun safes) may add to the price. We tell you before you book.
